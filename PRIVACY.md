# Privacy Policy — Interpreter Trainer

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Interpreter Trainer is developed by Zouhair Elachaqi. This policy describes how the Android application handles information.

## Practice data on your device

Saved practice sessions can include language choices, source details, transcripts, notes, feedback, timing information and audio recordings. This data is stored in the app's private storage on the device. Interpreter Trainer disables Android cloud backup for application data.

Deleting a saved session deletes its database record and any app-owned recording attached to it. Unreferenced app recordings are also pruned after a safety period. Uninstalling the app normally removes its private local data.

Interpreter Trainer does not include advertising, an owner-operated analytics SDK or an owner-operated tracking service.

## Optional online Interpreter AI

Interpreter AI is optional and requires an internet connection. Before it is enabled, the app displays an in-app disclosure and asks the user to continue or go back.

When a user opens and uses Interpreter AI, the following information may be sent to Puter and the selected hosted Qwen model to answer the request:

- messages entered in the coach;
- source and trainee material submitted for an evaluation; and
- up to five recent practice summaries, which may contain language pairs, durations, notes and previously saved feedback;
- AI reply text submitted for neural speech generation when voice output is used; and
- a voice-turn recording submitted for online transcription only when the device speech recognizer is unavailable or unstable.

Puter handles user authentication and AI access. Puter's terms, privacy policy and the policies of its underlying model providers apply to information processed by those services. Interpreter Trainer does not embed the developer's private AI API key.

The app stores coach cookies and web storage on the device to support authentication and continuity. The **Privacy & data** screen can clear the app's AI consent, cookies and embedded web storage. This local action does not delete a Puter account or information retained by an external provider; users must use the provider's account controls for those requests.

## Microphone, speech and media

Microphone permission is requested only for recording, transcription or AI voice features initiated by the user. Android speech recognition may process audio through the speech service configured on the device, which can be an online service. If that recognizer is unavailable or unstable during Interpreter Live, the app can record one voice turn and submit it through Puter for online transcription. AI reply text is submitted through Puter for neural text-to-speech, with Android text-to-speech as a fallback. The selected voice profile is stored locally in embedded web storage.

If a user opens an external media or webpage link, that third-party site or media host can receive ordinary network information such as the device IP address and may apply its own cookies and privacy policy. Interpreter Trainer does not bypass access controls on those sites.

## Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information. Data is disclosed to online providers only as needed for user-requested AI, speech, media or authentication functionality, or when required by law.

## Children

Interpreter Trainer is a professional training tool and is not directed to children under 13. Users must meet any minimum age required by Puter and other online services they choose to use.
